If you're here, you probably have money that could go a lot of places. And you've probably seen what happens when capital shapes mission instead of serving it: companies that started with values and lost them the moment investors needed returns, "impact" that was really marketing.

Worth Choosing is a mission, not a company. It does not take money, and this page does not ask for any. What it can do is tell you whether you are aligned, and where to go if you are.

## The filter

- **Patient timelines.** This isn't a quick flip. If you need returns in 18 months, we're not a fit.
- **Mission-lock valued as protection.** The structures mean the mission can't be sold out from under you or us.
- **No control needed.** You're backing something you believe in, not acquiring influence over its direction.
- **Money you can afford to lose.** This is early. The risk is real. If it doesn't make it, that capital is gone, and honesty requires naming it.

Read the [Vivid Vision](/vivid-vision/) and the [Values](/values/) before anything else. Alignment matters more than scale.
